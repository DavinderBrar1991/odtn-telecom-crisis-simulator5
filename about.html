About This Project

This website was created as part of the Operation Defend the North (ODTN) cybersecurity project. It simulates a nationwide cyberattack against a telecommunications provider and demonstrates how an Incident Response Team detects, contains, investigates, and recovers from the attack.

Scenario

The selected ODTN scenario is a large-scale cyberattack against a telecommunications company. The attack disrupts mobile communications, banking, healthcare, government services, and emergency response systems. The organization activates its Cyber Incident Response Plan, isolates affected infrastructure, preserves evidence, and restores critical services.

Target Audience

🎓 Cybersecurity Students
💻 IT Professionals
🛡 Incident Response Teams
🏢 Business Leaders

Purpose of the Website

The purpose of this website is to educate users about cybersecurity incident response through an interactive learning experience. Visitors can review the executive dashboard, understand the timeline of the attack, and participate in the Crisis Simulator to practice decision-making during a cyber incident.

Lessons Learned

✔ Early detection reduces damage.
✔ Business Continuity Planning is essential.
✔ Regular incident response training improves readiness.
✔ Continuous monitoring strengthens cyber resilience.
